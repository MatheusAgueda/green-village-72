# R31 — Recognisable animated circuit symbols

The customer requests recognisable lightning bolts moving along electrical wires and obvious water movement through pipes. R30's small highlights do move but are too subtle at the overview scale.

Add sparse, camera-facing vector lightning bolts and water drops, anchored to the existing routes and moving by arc length. Preserve the continuous in-pipe highlights, supply/drain directions, shared-trunk deduplication, play/pause/speed, reduced-motion preference, offscreen suspension and export freeze. Symbols must remain readable at overview and zoom without growing excessively or revealing circuits through opaque walls. Do not alter routing, commercial choices or prices.

Acceptance: symbols recognisable in desktop/mobile screenshots; successive frame positions differ during play and remain fixed during pause; symbols stay on routes through bends; duplicate branches do not multiply markers; allocations/disposal remain bounded; browser reports no WebGL/JS errors; published assets match the tested files.
